About Tyala Africa

Tyala Africa summarises historical crop evidence from public agricultural records for your area. It does not provide live weather, farm-level recommendations, yield predictions, or future forecasts.

Planting windows come from historical planting calendars. Prevalence is a share of reported cropped area. Both describe what was recorded in the past, not what is best or suitable for your farm.
